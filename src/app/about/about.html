About The Meeting

A gospel platform helping people locate their place in the plans of God through His Word.

Our Mission

We bring the Word of God to students and believers for conviction and edification, stirring hunger for Christ and helping people pursue the knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To preach the gospel to the unsaved and teach believers to observe all Christ has taught, structuring lives, words, and character to reflect the fullness of Christ.

Our Values

We value intentional, Scripture-centred discipleship that transforms lives, character, and conduct into the likeness of Christ.

Who We Are

The Meeting is not a ministry. It is a non-denominational and interdenominational platform that reaches out to the world and believers to preach the gospel of Christ and help people locate their place in the plans of God through the Word of God.

With youths as a major focus, we engage in campus and church youth fellowship invasions, conferences, seminars, outreaches, and meetings. We also follow up with students through physical meetings and platforms such as Facebook and WhatsApp.

Our work is centred on discipleship: the process of shaping the life, word, and character of believers to align with the Word of God.
