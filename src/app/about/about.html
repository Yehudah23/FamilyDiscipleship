About The Meeting

A gospel platform helping people locate their place in the plans of God through His Word.

Our Mission

We bring the Word of God to students and believers for conviction and edification, stirring hunger for Christ and helping people pursue the knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To preach the gospel to the unsaved and teach believers to observe all Christ has taught, structuring lives, words, and character to reflect the fullness of Christ.

Our Values

We value intentional, Scripture-centred discipleship that transforms lives, character, and conduct into the likeness of Christ.

Our Story

The Meeting is not a ministry, it is a non-denominational and interdenominational platform that reaches out to the world and believers to preach the gospel of Christ and help people locate their place in the plans of God through the Word of God (discipleship). Making youths the major focus, we engage in campuses and churches’ youth fellowship invasions, conferences, seminars, outreaches and meetings.

The Meeting began in 2015 with two young students at Kwara State College of Education, Oro, who shared a desire to know God more deeply. What started as personal fellowship and a mutual commitment to growing in the knowledge of God soon developed into the idea of gathering people around them for Bible study. Within the same year, two more students joined the movement, and together, they began drawing others into prayer and the study of God's Word. Gradually, it became a platform where people could encounter the truth of Scripture, grow in their faith, and receive personal follow-up.

As the vision grew, The Meeting moved beyond gathering people in groups to embracing intentional, one-on-one discipleship, evangelism, and personal spiritual growth. The conviction was that Christian maturity requires more than listening to God's Word in a gathering; it also requires personal guidance, accountability, and a commitment to living in accordance with Scripture. This vision eventually expanded beyond one institution, inspiring us to reach out to different campuses, churches, and fellowships with the gospel of Jesus Christ, while discipling believers who would, in turn, disciple others towards maturity in Christ.

Today, The Meeting remains committed to this vision: proclaiming Christ, grounding people in God's Word, and raising disciples who will faithfully follow Christ and help others do the same.
