Our Mission & Vision

Building families rooted in faith, growing together in love

Our Mission

To equip and empower families to live out their God-given purpose through biblical discipleship, authentic community, and intentional spiritual formation. We believe strong families build strong communities.

Our Vision

To see every family in our community transformed by the gospel, walking in unity, and raising up the next generation of faithful disciples who impact the world for Christ's kingdom.

Our Values

Faith-centered living, authentic relationships, generational discipleship, servant leadership, and sacrificial love. These values guide every program, message, and interaction in our ministry.

Our Story

Family Discipleship Ministry began in 2015 when a small group of families recognized a deep need in their community: parents wanted to disciple their children but didn't know where to start. What began as a weekly Bible study in a living room has grown into a thriving ministry serving over 50 families.

Today, our team of {{ disciplersCount }} dedicated disciplers provides structured programs, resources, and community support to help families build lasting spiritual foundations. We've seen marriages strengthened, children come to faith, and families develop rhythms of prayer and worship that transform their homes.

Our approach is simple but powerful: we walk alongside families, providing biblical teaching, practical tools, and authentic community. We don't just teach principles—we live them together.

What We Believe

01

Scripture Authority

The Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God and our final authority for faith and practice.

02

Family Discipleship

Parents are the primary disciplers of their children; the church exists to equip and support them.

03

Authentic Community

We grow best in transparent, accountable relationships where we bear one another's burdens.

04

Generational Faith

We invest in the next generation, believing God's promises extend to our children and their children.

05

Servant Leadership

We lead by serving, following Christ's example of humility and sacrificial love.

06

Missional Living

Every family is called to be a light in their neighborhood, workplace, and sphere of influence.
